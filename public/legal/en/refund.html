Cancellation, withdrawal and refunds

Updated 10 October 2026

Cancelling the renewal

You can cancel whenever you like, at no cost: in Ling, Account → Subscription → Turn off auto-renewal, or by writing to hi@ling.uno. You will not be charged for the next period and you keep access until the end of the period already paid for.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You may withdraw from the contract within 14 calendar days of paying for your first subscription, without giving a reason. To do so, tell us before the deadline with a clear statement, for example an email to hi@ling.uno from your account’s address. You may use the model form below, but it is not required.

We will refund the full amount, without deducting the time used, within 14 calendar days of receiving your notice, using the same payment method you used. You will not pay anything for the refund. Paid access ends when the refund is made.

Renewals

Renewals do not start a new withdrawal period. Even so, if a renewal was charged and you have not used paid access since, write to us within 14 days and we will refund it.

If the Service does not work

If the Service does not work as it should and we do not fix it within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a price reduction or to terminate the contract with a refund of the unused part, under the legal guarantee of conformity.

Purchases on iPhone and iPad

Subscriptions bought inside the iOS app are charged by Apple: request cancellation and refunds from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How the refund arrives

Refunds are made through Stripe to the same payment method. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on the bank.
