Privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Ling (ling.uno and the Ling app) processes, why, and what rights you have, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

1. Controller

Mariia Syrovatka, NIE [NIE], Calle D. Juan Antonio Baldovi 15, 8B, 03502 Benidorm (Alicante), España. Email: hi@ling.uno.

2. What we process, why, and on what basis

  • Account: your email address and sign-in data. To give you access to the Service. Basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your content: books you upload, saved words, reading progress and settings. To store them and sync them across your devices. Basis: performance of the contract.
  • Subscription and payments: plan, dates, status and Stripe’s customer, payment and subscription identifiers. Card details are processed by Stripe; we never receive them. To provide paid access and meet accounting and tax obligations. Basis: performance of the contract and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Visit statistics: a random identifier stored in your browser, the page visited, the referring website, the device type (mobile or desktop) and, if you are signed in, your account. To understand how the Service is used and improve it. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time in the cookie policy.
  • Communications: messages you send us. To reply. Basis: performance of the contract or legitimate interest.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, make automated decisions about you or profile you.

3. Who receives the data

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): database and accounts, with servers in the European Union (Stockholm). Processor.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland): processes payments. Stripe also acts as a controller to prevent fraud and meet its legal obligations; see stripe.com/privacy.
  • Web hosting provider: serves the site’s pages and logs technical connection data (such as the IP address).
  • Translation and dictionary services: when you translate a word, your browser sends it to Google Translate (Google LLC), MyMemory (Translated S.r.l., Italy) or Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation, Inc.). They receive the word and your IP address, but not your account.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): serves the site’s fonts and receives your IP address.
  • Apple: only if you subscribe in the iOS app, as the party charging you.
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account data and your content, for as long as you have the account. If you delete it, we erase them within 30 days.
  • Payment and billing data, for as long as the law requires (up to 6 years under Spanish commercial and tax law).
  • Visit statistics, for at most 2 years.

5. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing by writing to hi@ling.uno. To delete your account and all its data, write to us from the account’s email address. We reply within one month.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the authority of your country.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly process their data.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), database access is restricted to each account, and payment data is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS certified.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you by email or in the Service.
