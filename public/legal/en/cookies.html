Cookie and local storage policy

Updated 10 October 2026

Ling uses no cookies of its own and no advertising cookies. To work, it stores some data in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), which the law treats like cookies (Art. 22(2) of Spanish Law 34/2002, LSSI-CE).

Technical storage (necessary)

It needs no consent, because without it the Service does not work or does not remember your choices:

  • your account session, so you do not have to sign in on every visit;
  • the interface language, settings and whether you have seen the introduction;
  • books, words and reading progress in the browser, especially if you use Ling without an account.

Visit statistics (first-party)

We store a random identifier (ling-visitor) to count visits and unique visitors. Only we process this data, for statistics and in aggregate; it is not shared with third parties or used for advertising. You can turn it off in this browser:

Third parties

  • Stripe’s payment page (checkout.stripe.com) uses its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud: see stripe.com/cookies-policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts; it sets no cookies but receives your IP address.

How to delete this data

You can clear local storage in your browser settings (site data). This signs you out and removes settings saved in that browser.
